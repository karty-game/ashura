## Featured effects

- **Solar Burst** sends debris and smoke in all directions while its shock ring
  travels along the floor. **Hit Flash** and **Pickup Sparkle** scatter particles
  through spheres. **Ion Plume** and **Water Splash** use upward cones.
- **Arcane Portal** uses radial emission around a flat opening, with haze and
  sparks on both sides.
  **Ice Shatter** fans out two angular chip shapes with baked facets, frost and glints.
- **Fireworks** launches a shell with a spark trail before the three-color burst.
  **Water Splash** sends streaks upward and plays three timed landing ripples.
  **Waterfall Mist** starts with a full stream, then rebounds in low mist and spray.
  The falling streaks turn with their velocity as you orbit.
- **Fire** has a broad, turbulent base with rising embers and soot. **Torch Flame**
  has a narrow base, warm flickering tips and fewer sparks.
